FAQ

How is the 20% price calculated? Our price = official reference price × group multiplier 0.2; prices on the pricing page already include it. Input, output and cache reads are billed separately.

Which clients work? Anything that speaks the OpenAI or Anthropic API — Claude Code, Codex, CC Switch, OpenCode, Cherry Studio and the OpenAI SDK.

Are these original GPT / Claude models? No. "OpenAI" and "Anthropic" refer to compatible protocols; the models are DeepSeek, GLM, Kimi, MiniMax and Hunyuan.
